Anatomy of Nuit d’Ambre

A scent unfolds
in three acts.

I — Top notes

The opening

Bergamot from Calabria and pink pepper, bright for the first fifteen minutes. A spark before the warmth.

  • Bergamot
  • Pink pepper
  • Cardamom

II — Heart notes

The heart

Labdanum and amber resin settle in. Smoked oud adds depth without heaviness. This is the scent people will remember.

  • Amber resin
  • Labdanum
  • Oud

III — Base notes

The trail

Madagascan vanilla, tonka and soft sandalwood stay close to the skin until morning.

  • Vanilla
  • Tonka bean
  • Sandalwood

Sourced, not synthesised

Where our notes are grown.

  1. 01

    Bergamot

    Reggio Calabria, Italy

    Cold-pressed from the peel in December, when the fruit is still green and sharp.

  2. 02

    Iris

    Florence, Italy

    The root is dried for three years before it gives up its powdery, violet-like butter.

  3. 03

    Rose

    Grasse, France

    Picked by hand at dawn in May, before the sun burns off the scent.

  4. 04

    Oud

    Assam, India

    Resinous heartwood from agar trees, distilled slowly over several days.

  5. 05

    Vanilla

    Sava, Madagascar

    Pods are cured in the sun for months until they turn dark, soft and sweet.

The Atelier

Slow perfumery, on purpose.

We make fewer bottles than we could. Every formula is mixed by hand and left to mature for eight weeks before it is filtered, chilled and poured. Nothing is rushed, because good perfume can’t be.
