**Title:** Why you put things off, even with weeks to spare

**Description:**
```
Why do you put things off when the deadline is still far away? In a classic study, researchers followed college students over a semester and compared those who procrastinated with those who did not. Procrastinators felt less stress early in the term. But later they reported more stress and more symptoms of physical illness, and they earned lower grades. The authors called it the costs and benefits of dawdling: relief now, a bill later. It is one study of students, so treat it as a sign, not proof.

Source: Tice & Baumeister, 1997, Psychological Science, "Longitudinal study of procrastination, performance, stress, and health: The costs and benefits of dawdling".
```
