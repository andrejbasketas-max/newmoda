**Title:** Why you overthink at 3 a.m.

**Description:**
```
Why does a small worry feel enormous in the middle of the night? Researchers have proposed the "mind after midnight" hypothesis: in the early hours, thinking tends to lean negative and judgment gets weaker. It is a hypothesis, not a settled fact.

Source: Tubbs, Perlis & Basner, "Mind After Midnight: Nocturnal Wakefulness, Behavioral Dysregulation, and Psychopathology", Frontiers in Network Physiology.
```
