# A8

**Title:** What happens to your brain after just 4 days of meditation?

**Description:**
```
Can a few days of meditation change how your brain works? In one study, 49 students either learned mindfulness meditation or listened to The Hobbit read aloud, in four 20-minute sessions over four days. Both groups felt better in mood, but only the meditation group improved on tests of working memory and executive function. It is a small study and the author said you need to keep practicing, so treat it as a promising sign, not proof.

Source: Zeidan, Johnson, Diamond, David & Goolkasian, 2010, Consciousness and Cognition, "Mindfulness meditation improves cognition: Evidence of brief mental training".
```
