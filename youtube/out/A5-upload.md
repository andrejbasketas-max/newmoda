**Title:** What happens if you end every shower with cold water?

**Description:**
```
Dutch researchers asked more than three thousand adults to end their showers with 30 to 90 seconds of cold water for a month. They took about 29% fewer sick days off work, but they were not actually sick less often. One idea: the cold may simply have helped people feel able to get on with their day.

Source: Buijze et al., 2016, PLOS ONE ("The Effect of Cold Showering on Health and Work: A Randomized Controlled Trial").

#shorts #psychology #health
```
