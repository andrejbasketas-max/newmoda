**Title:** Learning to juggle: what happens to your brain?

**Description:**
```
What happens to your brain when you practice a brand new skill for three months? In one study, 24 volunteers who had never juggled were split into two groups, and one group learned a three-ball juggling routine over three months. Brain scans were taken before training, after three months, and after three more months without practice. After training, the jugglers showed a temporary increase in grey matter in regions involved in processing visual motion and space. That increase decreased again after they stopped practicing. It is a small study, so treat it as a sign, not proof.

Source: Draganski, Gaser, Busch, Schuierer, Bogdahn & May, 2004, Nature, "Neuroplasticity: changes in grey matter induced by training".
```
