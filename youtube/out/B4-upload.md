# B4

**Title:** Why you remember embarrassing moments forever

**Description:**
```
You forgot what you ate last Tuesday, but you will never forget that one awkward sentence. Psychologists call part of this negativity bias: bad events tend to weigh more than good ones, and emotional moments are stored more strongly in memory.

Sources: Baumeister et al., 2001, Review of General Psychology ("Bad is stronger than good"); McGaugh, 2004, Annual Review of Neuroscience ("The amygdala modulates the consolidation of memories of emotionally arousing experiences").

#shorts #psychology #memory
```
