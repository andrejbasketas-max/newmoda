**Title:** Alone with your thoughts: why it feels so hard

**Description:**
```
Could you sit alone in a quiet room for 15 minutes with nothing to do but think? In a famous set of 11 studies, most people did not enjoy it. In one of them, volunteers could press a button to give themselves a mild electric shock, even though they had said earlier they would pay to avoid it. About two thirds of the men and about a quarter of the women shocked themselves at least once. Other researchers argue the study shows less than it seems, because the shock was rated only mildly unpleasant, so the idea is still debated.

Sources: Wilson et al., 2014, Science, "Just think: The challenges of the disengaged mind"; Fox, Thompson, Andrews-Hanna & Christoff, 2014, Frontiers in Psychology, "Is thinking really aversive? A commentary on Wilson et al.'s 'Just think: The challenges of the disengaged mind'".
```
