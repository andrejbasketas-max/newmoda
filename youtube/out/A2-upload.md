**Title:** What happens to your brain if you sleep 6 hours a night?

**Description:**
```
You feel fine on six hours of sleep. That is exactly the problem. In one well-known study, people kept to six hours a night for two weeks tested like people who had skipped sleep for up to two nights.

Source: Van Dongen et al., 2003, Sleep ("The cumulative cost of additional wakefulness").

```
