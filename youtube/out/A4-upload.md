**Title:** What happens to your brain after 10 hours alone?

**Description:**
```
What happens to your brain after ten hours with no social contact? In a brain imaging study, people who were isolated showed a craving-like response in the midbrain when they saw pictures of people together, similar to the response to food after fasting.

Source: Tomova et al., 2020, Nature Neuroscience ("Acute social isolation evokes midbrain craving responses similar to hunger").

```
