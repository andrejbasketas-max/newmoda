**Title:** Bored out of your mind: what happens to creativity?

**Description:**
```
What happens to your creativity after a really boring task? In a pair of experiments, volunteers did a deliberately boring activity, such as copying or reading numbers from a phone book, and then took a creativity test, such as thinking of uses for plastic cups. The bored volunteers came up with more creative ideas than a control group that skipped the boring task. The authors suggest the dull, passive task let people daydream, and the daydreaming fed their creativity. It was a small lab study, so treat it as a sign, not proof.

Source: Mann & Cadman, 2014, Creativity Research Journal, "Does Being Bored Make Us More Creative?".

```
