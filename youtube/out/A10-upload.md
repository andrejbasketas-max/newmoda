**Title:** Holding a grudge: what happens to your body?

**Description:**
```
What happens to your body when you replay an old grudge? In one lab study, 71 volunteers recalled a real hurt and then thought about it in two different ways: nursing the grudge, or trying to see the other person with empathy and imagining forgiving them. While rehearsing the hurt, people felt more negative, angry and sad and less in control, and their blood pressure rose more. This was only a few minutes in a lab, not years, and it is one small study, so treat it as a sign, not proof.

Source: Witvliet, Ludwig & Vander Laan, 2001, Psychological Science, "Granting Forgiveness or Harboring Grudges: Implications for Emotion, Physiology, and Health".

```
