**Title:** A year of walking: what happens to your brain?

**Description:**
```
What happens to your brain if you walk for 40 minutes, three days a week, for a whole year? In one randomized trial, 120 older adults either walked or did stretching and toning. Scans showed the hippocampus, a region that helps form memories, grew by about 2 percent in the walking group, while it shrank by about 1.4 percent in the stretching group, which is roughly the normal loss with age. Both groups improved on a spatial memory test, but the walking group improved more. It is one study of older adults, so treat it as a promising sign, not proof for everyone.

Source: Erickson et al., 2011, PNAS, "Exercise training increases size of hippocampus and improves memory".

```
