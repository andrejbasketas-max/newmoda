**Title:** Why you crave junk food (your Stone Age brain)

**Description:**
```
Why is junk food so hard to stop eating? One idea is that your brain was shaped when calorie-rich food was rare, so it rewards you for finding it. In a controlled study at the U.S. National Institutes of Health, 20 adults ate ultra-processed meals for two weeks and unprocessed meals for two weeks, with meals matched for sugar, fat, fiber and sodium. On the ultra-processed diet they ate about 500 more calories a day and gained around two pounds. The Stone Age part is a hypothesis; the study is small and was done in a clinic.

Source: Hall et al., 2019, Cell Metabolism, "Ultra-Processed Diets Cause Excess Calorie Intake and Weight Gain: An Inpatient Randomized Controlled Trial of Ad Libitum Food Intake".
```
