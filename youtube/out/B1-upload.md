**Title:** Why your brain keeps making you check your phone

**Description:**
```
Why do you keep checking your phone when nothing new happened? Psychologists point to variable rewards: most checks give you nothing, some give you something good, and that unpredictability keeps the habit going.

Sources: Ferster & Skinner, 1957, Schedules of Reinforcement; Oulasvirta et al., 2012, Personal and Ubiquitous Computing ("Habits make smartphone use more compulsive").

```
