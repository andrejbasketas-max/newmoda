**Title:** What happens to your brain after reading a novel for 9 days?

**Description:**
```
What happens to your brain when you read a novel for nine days? In one small study, 21 students read the same novel over nine evenings while researchers scanned their brains each morning. The scans showed stronger connectivity in a part of the left temporal cortex linked to language and in a region linked to bodily sensation, and some of the changes were still there days after the book ended. It is a small study, so treat it as a promising sign, not proof.

Source: Berns, Blaine, Prietula & Pye, 2013, Brain Connectivity ("Short- and Long-Term Effects of a Novel on Connectivity in the Brain").
```
